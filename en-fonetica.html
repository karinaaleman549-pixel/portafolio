General description

This technical guide gathers the symbols of the International Phonetic Alphabet (IPA) used in American English pronunciation. It includes vowels, consonants, diphthongs, r-colored vowels (vowels with /r/), and common phonological rules, such as aspiration, the Flap T, and the Glottal T.

Key concepts

Flap T

The /t/ between voiced sounds is pronounced as a quick tap similar to a /d/.

Water – [ˈwɑɾɚ]

Glottal T

The /t/ before a weak syllable with /n/ can become a glottal stop.

Button – [ˈbʌʔn̩]

Dark L

Final /l/ slightly changes the position of the preceding vowel.

Examples: call, bold…

Aspiration

The consonants /p, t, k/ at the beginning of stressed words are produced with a strong burst of air.

Examples: play, lap…
